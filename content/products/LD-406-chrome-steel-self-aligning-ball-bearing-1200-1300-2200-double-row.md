  - question: "When should I choose a tapered bore (K suffix) over a cylindrical bore?"
    answer: "Choose a tapered bore with K suffix when you need to mount the bearing directly on a tapered shaft or when using an adapter sleeve for mounting on a straight shaft. Tapered bore designs allow for easier mounting and dismounting, provide better radial clearance adjustment, and are preferred in applications requiring frequent maintenance or where precise bearing positioning is needed."
  - question: "What is the load capacity of double row self-aligning ball bearings compared to single row bearings?"
    answer: "Double row self-aligning ball bearings have significantly higher radial load capacity than single row designs, typically 1.5 to 2 times the capacity of comparable single row bearings. The double row arrangement also provides improved axial load capability in both directions. However, they have lower speed limits than single row deep groove ball bearings due to the increased friction from the spherical raceway geometry."
