faqdata:
  - question: "How do I choose between open, ZZ, and 2RS bearing types?"
    answer: "Open bearings are for enclosed housings with external lubrication and offer the highest speed capability. ZZ shielded bearings provide basic contamination protection with metal shields that do not contact the inner ring, suitable for moderately dirty environments. 2RS rubber-sealed bearings have contact seals that provide superior protection against dust, dirt, and moisture, ideal for exposed or outdoor applications but with slightly lower speed ratings."
  - question: "What is the difference between 6000, 6200, and 6300 series bearings?"
    answer: "The three series represent different load capacities within the same bore size. The 6000 series is the light series with the smallest cross-section and lowest load rating. The 6200 series is the medium series and the most commonly used, offering a good balance of load capacity and compactness. The 6300 series is the heavy series with the widest width, largest balls, and highest radial and axial load capacity."
  - question: "What does ABEC precision grade mean and which do I need?"
    answer: "ABEC (Annular Bearing Engineers Committee) grades define the dimensional tolerance and running accuracy of bearings. ABEC 1 is the standard commercial grade suitable for most general-purpose applications. ABEC 3 offers improved precision for moderate-speed equipment. ABEC 5 provides high precision for applications requiring smooth rotation at higher speeds. Higher ABEC grades are typically needed for machine tools, high-speed spindles, and precision instruments."
  - question: "What is the recommended lubrication and relubrication interval?"
    answer: "Most sealed bearings (2RS) are pre-lubricated with lithium-based grease and are maintenance-free for their service life. Open bearings can be lubricated with either grease or oil depending on the application. Relubrication intervals depend on operating conditions such as speed, temperature, and contamination levels. As a general guideline, grease-lubricated bearings should be relubricated every 1000-2000 hours of operation under normal conditions."
