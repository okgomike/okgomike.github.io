The flare nut wrench grips five sides, distributing the torque much more evenly and preventing damage.   - question: "How do I use a flare nut wrench with tubing attached?"
    answer: "Slide the wrench over the tubing through the open slot in the box-end, then position the wrench on the flare nut. The opening allows the wrench to pass over the tubing while the 5-point grip engages the nut. Tighten or loosen the nut with steady pressure. For stubborn flare nuts, apply penetrating oil and allow it to soak before attempting to loosen. Avoid excessive force that could twist or kink the tubing."
