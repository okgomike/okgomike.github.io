  - question: "What is the difference between copper and rubber swivel pads?"
    answer: "Copper pads are used for welding to prevent spatter from sticking to the pad and to protect the workpiece from spark damage. Rubber pads are used for delicate surfaces like finished wood or painted metal to prevent marring. Plain steel pads are for general-purpose use on rugged materials where surface marking is not a concern."
  - question: "Can C-clamps be used for overhead lifting?"
    answer: "No, C-clamps are designed for clamping and holding workpieces, not for lifting. Never use a C-clamp to suspend or lift loads overhead. Use certified lifting clamps or hoists rated for lifting applications. C-clamp failure under load can result in serious injury."
  - question: "How do I maintain my C-clamps?"
