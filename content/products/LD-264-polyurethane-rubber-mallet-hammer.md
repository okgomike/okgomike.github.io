  - question: "What is the difference between a rubber mallet and a dead blow mallet?"
    answer: "A standard rubber mallet rebounds (bounces back) upon impact, which can reduce the effective striking force and cause the mallet to bounce in tight spaces. A dead blow mallet contains steel shot or sand inside the head that shifts upon impact, absorbing the rebound energy and delivering all the force to the workpiece. Dead blow mallets are preferred for precise positioning tasks and when working in confined spaces. Both types are non-marring."
