  - question: "How do I install a surface-mounted barrel bolt correctly?"
    answer: "Start by positioning the bolt body on the door or gate at your desired height, typically 10-15cm from the top or bottom edge for best security. Mark the screw holes through the mounting plate. Pre-drill pilot holes slightly smaller than the screw diameter to prevent wood splitting. Secure the bolt body with the provided screws. Close the door or gate and mark the position of the strike plate on the frame, ensuring the bolt pin aligns perfectly with the strike plate opening. Install the strike plate with screws. Test the bolt operation by sliding it back and forth several times; it should move smoothly and engage fully with the strike plate. If the bolt binds, adjust the strike plate position slightly. For padlock loop versions, verify that the padlock can be inserted and removed easily when the bolt is in the locked position."
