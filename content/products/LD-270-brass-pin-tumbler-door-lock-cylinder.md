  - question: "What is the difference between keyed alike and keyed different?"
    answer: "Keyed alike (KA) means all cylinders in a set operate with the same key. This is convenient for homeowners who want one key to open all doors. Keyed different (KD) means each cylinder has its own unique key. This is appropriate for commercial applications where different users need access to different areas. A master key system allows both individual keys and a master key to operate cylinders, providing hierarchical access control."
  - question: "Can I rekey these cylinders myself?"
    answer: "Rekeying requires disassembling the cylinder, replacing the pins with a new pinning configuration, and reassembling. This requires a rekeying kit with assorted pins, a plug follower tool, and knowledge of the keyway and pinning system. For most users, it is more practical to purchase new cylinders keyed to the desired configuration. Professional locksmiths can rekey these cylinders quickly and inexpensively. The cylinders are designed to be rekeyable for maintenance and security updates."

    answer: "Measure the diameter of the existing cylinder hole in the lock or door. Measure the length of the existing cylinder from the front face to the back of the body. The cam or tailpiece length should also match.
