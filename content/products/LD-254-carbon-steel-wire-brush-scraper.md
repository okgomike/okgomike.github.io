For stainless steel surfaces, use stainless steel wire to prevent contamination. For delicate surfaces or spark-sensitive areas, use brass wire for gentle cleaning."
  - question: "What is the difference between carbon steel and stainless steel wire brushes?"
    answer: "Carbon steel wire brushes are more aggressive and economical, ideal for cleaning carbon steel and iron surfaces. However, carbon steel bristles can leave residue that causes rust on stainless steel surfaces. Stainless steel wire brushes prevent this contamination and are required for cleaning stainless steel, aluminum, and other non-ferrous metals. Never use carbon steel brushes on stainless steel."
  - question: "How long do wire brushes last?"
    answer: "Brush life depends on usage intensity and surface hardness. For light rust removal on soft metals, a brush can last several months of regular use. For heavy-duty rust removal on hard steel, brushes may need replacement after a few hours of intensive use. Replace brushes when bristles become significantly shortened, bent, or fatigued. A worn brush is less effective and may shed bristles."
  - question: "Can I use a wire brush on wood surfaces?"
    answer: "Yes, wire brushes are effective for removing old paint and varnish from wood, creating distressed textures, and cleaning weathered wood surfaces.
