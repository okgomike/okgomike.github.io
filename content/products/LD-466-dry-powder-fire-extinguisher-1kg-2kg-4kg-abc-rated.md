faqdata:
  - question: "What is the difference between an ABC dry powder extinguisher and a CO2 extinguisher?"
    answer: "An ABC dry powder extinguisher uses ammonium phosphate powder that interrupts the fire's chemical chain reaction and works on Class A, B, and C fires (ordinary combustibles, flammable liquids/gases, and electrical equipment). A CO2 extinguisher uses carbon dioxide gas that displaces oxygen and is mainly used for Class B and electrical fires, leaving no residue. ABC powder is more versatile but leaves a messy residue, while CO2 is clean but less effective on Class A fires."
