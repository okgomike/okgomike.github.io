  - question: "What is the difference between single cylinder and double cylinder deadbolts?"
    answer: "A single cylinder deadbolt has a key cylinder on the outside and a thumbturn knob on the inside. You lock and unlock it from the outside with a key and from the inside by turning the thumbturn. This is the most common type for residential use and provides quick egress in emergencies. A double cylinder deadbolt has key cylinders on both sides, requiring a key to lock or unlock from either side. Double cylinder provides higher security against intruders reaching through broken glass, but may pose an egress hazard in emergencies. Always check local building codes before installing double cylinder deadbolts, as many areas require single cylinder on egress doors for life safety."
