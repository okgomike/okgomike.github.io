  - question: "What does 95% filtration efficiency (N95/KN95/FFP2) mean?"
    answer: "A 95% efficiency rating means the respirator filters at least 95% of airborne non-oil-based particles down to 0.3 microns, which is the most penetrating particle size. N95 is the NIOSH standard, KN95 is the Chinese GB2626 standard, and FFP2 is the European EN149 standard. All three offer comparable particulate filtration performance."
  - question: "What is the advantage of the exhalation valve?"
    answer: "The exhalation valve allows warm, moist exhaled air to exit directly without passing back through the filter, reducing breathing resistance and heat buildup inside the mask. It also reduces fogging of safety glasses or goggles. It does not affect inward filtration, so the wearer is still protected against incoming particles, but valved masks should not be used in sterile environments where outward exhalation must be filtered."
  - question: "How do I ensure a proper fit and seal?"
    answer: "To ensure a proper seal, place the mask over the nose and mouth, pull the headbands over the head, and mold the adjustable aluminum nose clip firmly against the bridge of the nose. Perform a fit check by inhaling sharply to confirm the mask collapses slightly and no air leaks around the edges, and exhaling to confirm no air escapes around the nose. Beards, stubble, or facial hair can prevent an effective seal."
