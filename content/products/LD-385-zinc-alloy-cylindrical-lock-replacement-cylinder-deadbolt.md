    answer: "To find the right replacement cylinder, first identify your lock brand or keyway type by comparing your key to SC1, KW1, and Y1 key profiles. SC1 keys have a distinctive shape with a longer blade and specific cuts, while KW1 keys have a shorter, wider blade. Y1 keys have a unique Yale-style profile. Next, measure the cylinder diameter (typically 25-30mm for standard cylindrical locks) and the tailpiece length (usually 20-35mm). If you are still unsure, remove your existing cylinder and compare it to the product photos or send us photos and measurements and our technical team will help you identify the correct replacement."

  - question: "What is the difference between 5-pin and 6-pin cylinders?"
    answer: "The number of pins refers to the number of pin tumblers inside the lock cylinder. A 5-pin cylinder has five pairs of pins (top pins and bottom pins) that must be raised to the correct height by the key cuts to allow the cylinder to turn. A 6-pin cylinder has six pairs. More pins means more possible key combinations, which theoretically makes the lock more resistant to picking and bumping, and reduces the chance of accidental key duplication. 5-pin cylinders are the most common in residential applications and are compatible with standard SC1 and KW1 keyways. 6-pin cylinders offer higher security and are often used in commercial and institutional settings. When replacing a cylinder, match the pin count to your existing lock, or upgrade from 5-pin to 6-pin for enhanced security, keeping in mind that this will require new keys with the appropriate number of cuts."
